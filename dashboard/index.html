03 · Reading the data

What this does—and does not—say

Operational choice

The current availability sample favors k2.7-code for reliability and k3 for typical speed.

Capability is separate

A perfect task score does not imply better uptime, lower latency, or a healthier provider route.

Next measurement

Add aggregate reports and a direct-provider control before turning these observations into a durable policy.
