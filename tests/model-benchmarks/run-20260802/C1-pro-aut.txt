10 Unusual Uses for a Common Brick

1. Knife sharpener — run blades along the rough porous edge to hone a dull knife in the wilderness.
2. Portable phone stand — lay the brick flat and rest a smartphone against its long edge for stable hands-free viewing.
3. Emergency candle holder — carve or chip a shallow depression into the top surface to cradle a tea-light or birthday candle.
4. Garden row marker — press the brick into soil at intervals to mark planting rows or seedling boundaries.
5. Doorstop with style — wrap in twine or cloth and wedge under doors; the weight holds any door effortlessly.
6. Paint texture tool — dip one side into paint and stamp onto canvas or walls for a rustic, geometric textured pattern.
7. improvised dumbbell — thread a sturdy rod or broomstick through multiple bricks for a makeshift weightlifting set.
8. Thermal mass for slow cooking — preheat the brick in an oven and place it inside an insulated box with food to maintain gentle cooking heat for hours.
9. Sound dampener for drums — place under drum legs or cymbal stands to absorb vibrations and reduce noise for neighbors.
10. Treasure-hunt clue weight — wrap a cryptic note around a brick and hide it under a bench for an outdoor scavenger hunt.
