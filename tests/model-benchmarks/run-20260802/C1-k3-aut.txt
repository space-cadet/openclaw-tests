10 Unusual Uses for a Common Brick

1. Thermal battery — Heat bricks in a fire or oven, then place them in an insulated container to keep food warm for hours without electricity.
2. Acoustic diffuser — Mount bricks at varying angles on a wall to break up sound reflections and reduce echo in a room or recording space.
3. Impromptu anvil — Use a solid brick as a small, sturdy striking surface for light metalworking, jewelry making, or riveting tasks.
4. Garden edge marker — Bury bricks vertically in soil to create a decorative, durable border that prevents grass from creeping into flower beds.
5. Mortar and pestle base — Grind spices, grains, or pigments by rubbing them against the flat, gritty surface of a brick with a smaller stone.
6. Heat diffuser — Place a brick under a pot on a gas stove to absorb and distribute heat evenly, preventing hot spots and scorching.
7. Dumbbell core — Slide a brick onto a pipe or dowel to create a custom, adjustable weight for home workouts.
8. Spray paint stencil — Lay bricks on canvas or paper and spray over them to create textured, geometric patterns in street art or crafts.
9. Knife honing stone — Use the fine-grained surface of a smooth brick to touch up and realign a knife's edge when no proper hone is available.
10. Smartphone stand — Prop a phone at a comfortable viewing angle by leaning it against a brick tilted on its edge for hands-free video calls.
